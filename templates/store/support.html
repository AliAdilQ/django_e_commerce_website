HERE TO MAKE THINGS SIMPLE

A little help goes a long way.

Everything you need to know about exploring this demo store.

{% include 'includes/icon.html' with name='truck' %} Delivery

All demo orders include complimentary simulated delivery. NovaShop is a portfolio project: orders are stored locally, and no physical products are shipped.

Your order starts as Pending. A local administrator can update it to Processing, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled in the Django admin panel.

{% include 'includes/icon.html' with name='return' %} Returns & exchanges

Our fictional collection is built around easy returns. Since no real transactions occur, there are no real refunds or return shipments. To demonstrate a cancelled order, update its status in the admin panel. A status change does not automatically replenish stock.

{% include 'includes/icon.html' with name='shield' %} Payments & privacy

Choose Cash on Delivery or Demo Card Payment during checkout. Both options are simulated. We never ask for, store, or process real card details.

Your account, delivery details, orders, and newsletter subscription are stored in this installation's SQLite database. Use fictional personal details while testing. Newsletter subscriptions remain local; no emails are sent or shared with external services.

{% include 'includes/icon.html' with name='user' %} Accounts & assistance

Create an account to check out and view your order history. Guest shopping bags stay in your browser session. You can update your name and email in My Profile.

For project documentation, setup instructions, and source code, visit the GitHub repository.
